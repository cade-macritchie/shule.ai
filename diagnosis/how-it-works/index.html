How it works

From one conversation to work in good order.

What happens when you start, what your team is asked to do, and what you get at each step.

  1. 1
    5 minutes

    Tell us what to look at

    Fill in a short form: the processes you want documented, why they matter, and who knows them best. Attach any existing SOPs, screenshots or exports if you have them.

  2. 2
    Same day

    We invite your people

    Each person gets a friendly email explaining what to expect and a link to book a time that suits them. We send gentle reminders, so you don't have to chase anyone.

  3. 3
    About 45 minutes each

    A conversation with our AI interviewer

    They join from a link in their browser. No downloads, no Zoom. The interviewer asks how the work really happens: the steps, the systems, where things wait, and what goes wrong. They can share their screen to show it, and stop at any time.

  4. 4
    After each interview

    Your docs and diagnosis appear in your portal

    We turn the conversation into clear process docs and a Lean Six Sigma diagnosis of what is holding your constraint in place. That covers broken steps and missing practices: hiring that runs only on word of mouth is a cause, even if nothing is technically broken. Each cause gets a remedy, ranked by impact. Anything we couldn't confirm becomes a short question for your team.

  5. 5
    Free preview, then your choice

    Watch a free preview, unlock the remedy

    Each major problem comes with a free preview video that shows what's happening and why. When you want the fix, unlock the full remedy: the video guide, a step-by-step build guide, and a ready-to-paste prompt so your team can implement it with an AI assistant.

Broken, or missing?

Sometimes the process works fine and the constraint comes from something the school has never started doing. The diagnosis looks for both: what to fix, and what to begin.

Not enough students

Fix what's broken: Inquiries wait days for a first reply

Start what's missing: No follow-up after tours, and no ambassador families telling their friends

Students aren't profitable enough

Fix what's broken: Aid is decided case by case, late, by email

Start what's missing: No tuition model: nobody knows the real cost to serve one student

Can't find and keep good teachers

Fix what's broken: Applications sit in an inbox for two weeks

Start what's missing: Hiring runs on word of mouth: no LinkedIn presence, no Christian-college pipeline, no referral program

Can't match better-funded schools on experience

Fix what's broken: Parent questions bounce between the office and teachers

Start what's missing: No plan for a new family's first 90 days, so the welcome depends on who picks up the phone

Don't know what a student costs to win

Fix what's broken: Inquiry source is typed in three different ways

Start what's missing: Marketing and recruiting spend are never tied to the families they brought in
