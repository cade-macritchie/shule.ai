Common questions.

Is the interview and audit really free?

Yes. The interviews, docs, audit and preview videos cost nothing. You only pay to unlock a full remedy (video, build guide and AI prompt), and only for the ones you choose.

What if nothing is actually broken?

Then the diagnosis says so, and looks for what's missing instead. Often the constraint comes from a practice the school never started: teachers found only by word of mouth, no follow-up after tours, no tuition model. Those get a remedy too.

What's the AI prompt?

Each unlocked remedy includes a prompt written for that fix, with your systems, fields and rules filled in. Paste it into an AI assistant and it walks your team through building it, step by step.

What's in the free preview video?

The current state of the process, the wastes we found and the root cause, so you can see exactly what the problem is. It ends by listing what the full guide covers.

Why an AI interviewer?

It can meet each person when it suits them, ask patient follow-up questions, and turn the conversation into docs right away. People can end the interview at any time.

Who sees the recordings?

Only the people in your organization you give portal access to. Personal details on screen are blurred automatically, and raw recordings are deleted after 30 days.

Can we edit the docs?

Yes. Answer questions in the portal or approve pages as they are. Answers are written back into the docs.

What if someone isn't comfortable talking to an AI?

That's completely fine. They agree on screen before starting and can stop at any time, and nothing is recorded unless they begin.
